The International Council of Ophthalmology (ICO) exams are recognized globally. Understanding the format is the first step to preparation.

## ICO Basic Sciences (Part A)

80 MCQs covering anatomy, physiology, pathology, microbiology, and optics.

## ICO Optics and Refraction (Part B)

40 MCQs focused on optics, refraction, and clinical optics.

## ICO Clinical Science (Part C)

200 MCQs covering all clinical ophthalmology subspecialties.
