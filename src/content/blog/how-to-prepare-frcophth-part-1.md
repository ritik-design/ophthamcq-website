The FRCOphth Part 1 exam tests your knowledge of basic sciences applied to ophthalmology. With the right strategy, you can pass on your first attempt.

## Understand the Exam Format

The exam consists of two papers, each with 90 SBA questions. You must pass both papers in the same sitting. The pass mark typically ranges from 60–65%.

## Topic Weightage

- Optics: ~25%
- Pathology: ~20%
- Anatomy/Embryology: ~12%
- Physiology: ~12%
- Statistics: ~8–15%

## 6-Month Study Plan

Months 1–2: Build foundation in optics, pathology, and anatomy. Months 3–4: Cover all medium-weightage topics. Months 5–6: High-yield question practice and mock exams.
