Glaucoma is one of the most frequently tested topics in ophthalmology PG exams. These 50 MCQs cover open-angle, angle-closure, congenital glaucoma, and medical/surgical management.

## Question 1

A 60-year-old patient has IOP of 28 mmHg, open angles on gonioscopy, optic disc cupping of 0.7, and normal visual fields. What is the most likely diagnosis?

**Answer:** Primary open-angle glaucoma suspect. Normal tension glaucoma requires documented progression despite normal IOP.
