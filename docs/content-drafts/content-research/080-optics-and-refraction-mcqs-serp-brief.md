- How do I convert focal length to dioptres without a unit error?

- What is spherical equivalent and why does it not replace astigmatic correction?
- What changes in accommodation, presbyopia, myopia and hyperopia?
- How are prism dioptres, total internal reflection, chromatic aberration and spherical aberration tested?

## SERP / competitor read

The target query is served mainly by short quiz pages, formula sheets and general optometry teaching pages. Their recurring weakness is that they test a fact but do not show the procedural source of the error. This draft is differentiated by: (1) answer-first worked explanations, (2) a repeatable sign–units–formula sequence, (3) cylinder transposition verified in both principal meridians, and (4) a clear boundary between the exam model and clinical prescribing.

**Differentiation statement:** Rather than another unannotated optics question list, the page turns each error-prone optical rule into a short calculation or physical-sense check that a resident can reuse under SBA time pressure.
