## Evidence and boundary

[WHO: Burn-out](https://www.who.int/mental_health/evidence/burn-out/en/) returned HTTP 200 on 18 August 2026. It supports the ICD-11 scope distinction: burnout is an occupational phenomenon rather than a medical condition in that classification. It does **not** support diagnosis, self-assessment, treatment, crisis management or a claim that a particular study method prevents burnout.

The draft directs readers with safety concerns, self-harm thoughts, inability to function, or severe/persistent symptoms to urgent local professional/emergency support and a trusted person. It gives no therapeutic advice, medication advice, crisis protocol or service-specific contact number.
