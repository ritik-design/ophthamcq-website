## SERP gap and information gain

Search results often collapse FRCOphth, GMC registration and job searching into one supposed pathway. That framing can cause costly irrelevant preparation. The information gain is a four-track map, current-advert-first gap analysis, evidence map, clearly scoped 12-week planning template and same-day final-verification procedure.

**Differentiation statement:** A UK-route guide that explains which institution decides each question and refuses to turn an examination credential into a registration, visa or job guarantee.
