| [National Medical Commission](https://www.nmc.org.in/) | Registration/regulatory starting point | Fellowship placement or a hospital’s final eligibility decision |

The Aravind, LVPEI and AIOS endpoints returned HTTP 200 on 18 August 2026. The NMC endpoint was not reachable from this research environment (HTTP 000), so it is retained only as the named official regulatory starting point and is explicitly marked for final-editor recheck. Because programme openings and terms are mutable, the draft intentionally contains no unverified deadline, duration, stipend, seat count, selection rate, employer claim or “complete list” assertion. Final-editor rule: reopen the named institutional notice immediately before publication and again immediately before application.

## SERP gap and information gain

The query is saturated with aggregators and static “top fellowship” lists that mix old durations, fees, vacancies and admissions routes. The useful gap is not another hospital-name list; it is a method that lets a doctor create a dated, evidence-backed shortlist. The draft provides the fields to capture, official-domain search ladder, comparison questions, document checklist and a 24-hour final-verification rule.

**Differentiation statement:** A fellowship-search article that is honest about time-sensitive directory facts and gives readers a reproducible primary-source research workflow rather than fabricated completeness.
